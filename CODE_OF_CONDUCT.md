# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Keyina welcomes respectful participation from people of every background and experience level.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- Discussing ideas and code on their technical merits.
- Giving specific, actionable, and considerate feedback.
- Acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes openly.
- Respecting privacy, accessibility needs, and language differences.
- Keeping disagreements focused on the project rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or repeatedly disrupting project work.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces represented as part of the Keyina community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and focused on protecting contributors and the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Include links, dates, context, and any immediate safety concern. Do not use a public issue when doing so could expose a target to further harm.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is not permitted.
